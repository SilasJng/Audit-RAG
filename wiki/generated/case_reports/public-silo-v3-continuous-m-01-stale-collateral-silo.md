## Summary

Silo Enterprise continuous audit 报告中，借款人还清一侧债务后，borrowerCollateralSilo 未被重置；之后通过 debt-share transfer 接收另一侧债务时，solvency 配置仍解析到旧 collateral silo，使同资产抵押/债务按 1:1 假设估值。K2 应重点映射到 aToken/debt token transfer、position owner 转移、reserve id/bitmap/cache 未刷新。

## Broken invariants

- a borrower's solvency configuration must be derived from live debt and collateral positions after every debt-share transfer
- repaying one position must not leave stale collateral-side state that authorizes a later incompatible debt position
