## Summary

Attacker can make the CurveVolatileCollateral enter the status of IFFY/DISABLED. It will cause the basket to rebalance and sell off all the CurveVolatileCollateral.

## Broken invariants

- collateral status checks must not depend on instantly manipulable pool balances or spot prices
- flash-loan changes to a Curve volatile pool must not be able to mark collateral sound or unsound incorrectly
- default/delay logic must be based on manipulation-resistant prices
