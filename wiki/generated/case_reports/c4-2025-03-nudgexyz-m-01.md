## Summary

NudgeCampaign.handleReallocation trusts calls from an address with SWAP_CALLER_ROLE, but that role is granted to Li.Fi Executor, whose swapAndExecute entrypoint is permissionless. Attackers can route calls through Executor to create arbitrary or repeated reallocations. When malicious participations are invalidated, invalidateParticipations subtracts pendingRewards but does not fully restore the affected claimable/fee accounting, so repeated abuse can permanently reduce available rewards and disrupt legitimate users.

## Broken invariants

- only legitimate user-driven swaps should create campaign participations
- invalidating malicious participations must restore reward accounting consistently
- available campaign rewards should not be depleted by permissionless executor-mediated calls
