## Summary

Malicious users can transfer USDe token to StakedUSDe protocol directly lead to a denial of service (DoS) for StakedUSDe due to the limit shares check.

## Broken invariants

- direct underlying-token donations to the vault must not let an attacker make normal deposits fail their minimum-share checks
- share minting safeguards must tolerate unsolicited asset balance changes or enforce a minimum deposit that makes rounding griefing uneconomic
- vault accounting must distinguish intended deposits from direct token transfers when enforcing slippage/min-share constraints
