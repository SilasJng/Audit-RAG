## Summary

CTokenV3Collateral._underlyingRefPerTok uses erc20Decimals which is the decimals of CusdcV3Wrapper. But it should use the decimals of the underlying Comet.

## Broken invariants

- CTokenV3 collateral wrappers must use the underlying Comet asset decimals when converting refPerTok
- decimal assumptions must match the actual yield-bearing token accounting domain
- misconfigured decimal sources must not skew collateral valuation
