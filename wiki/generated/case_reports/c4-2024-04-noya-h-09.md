## Summary

While in Recovery Mode, if your vault’s Individual Collateral Ratio (ICR) falls below the GTCR, your vault can be liquidated (even if your vault’s collateral ratio is above 110%). To prevent this from happening, in both Normal and Recovery Mode, a user should maintain their collateral ratio over 150%.

## Broken invariants

- vault valuation must remain correct under the root cause: Prisma recovery-mode integration omits the surplus-claim path, leaving excess collateral unclaimable after liquidation/recovery conditions
- oracle, TVL, and collateral valuation must use correctly scaled, fresh, and manipulation-resistant inputs
- state changes must remain internally consistent across all affected accounting buckets and external integrations
