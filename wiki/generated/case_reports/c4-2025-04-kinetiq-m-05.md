## Summary

When a user stakes in StakingManager, initially the funds go towards hypeBuffer and when it is filled, every deposit is placed in a L1 operation queue.

## Broken invariants

- permissionless deposits must not allow an attacker to create an unprocessable L1 operation queue
- queue reset and processing must be bounded or batchable under realistic block gas limits
- minimum deposit size and batching rules must make gas-based griefing economically or technically infeasible
