## Summary

When unwrapping the wComet to its rebasing comet, users with an equivalent amount of wComet invoking CusdcV3Wrapper._withdraw at around the same time could end up having different percentage gains because comet is not linearly rebasing.

## Broken invariants

- wrapper withdrawals must validate external protocol state before moving user shares or assets
- Comet integration failures must not leave users with inconsistent wrapper accounting
- principal exits from wrappers need explicit protection separate from reward logic
