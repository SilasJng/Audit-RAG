## Summary

The way withdrawals and deposits are processed in processL1Operations (all withdrawals requests first, then all deposits) can lead in some cases, to balance not being delegated, which ultimately reduce earned rewards from validator, making the vault less profitable than expected.

## Broken invariants

- same-batch deposits and withdrawals should be netted where possible before issuing L1 operations
- processing order must not leave stakeable funds idle when offsetting withdrawal demand exists in the same batch
- L1 operation batching should preserve expected validator delegation and yield efficiency
