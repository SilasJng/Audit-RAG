## Summary

Reflector V3 在 asset registry 中维护资产列表与 expiration vector。零初始 expiration 的新增资产会造成两个向量长度不一致，后续 extend_ttl() 按资产索引读取 expiration 时 panic，导致维护或查询流程 DoS。

## Broken invariants

- every registered asset must have a corresponding expiration/TTL entry before indexed maintenance
- storage lifecycle maintenance must not panic on valid asset-addition parameters
