## Summary

The StakedUSDe contract implements a method to SOFTLY or FULLY restrict user address, and either transfer to another user or burn.

## Broken invariants

- a FULL_RESTRICTED staker must not be able to move value out through either direct calls or allowance-mediated ERC4626 withdraw/redeem paths
- restriction checks must cover caller, receiver, and token owner because ERC4626 allows approved third parties to withdraw on behalf of an owner
- approval-based flows must not create an alternate path around compliance transfer restrictions
