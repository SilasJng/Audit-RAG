## Summary

For cross-chain reallocations, handleReallocation uses a userAddress parameter sourced from the origin-chain account and transfers destination-chain toToken to that same address. Account-abstraction wallets such as Safe may have different addresses across chains. If a user initiates on one chain and receives on another, the destination transfer can go to an address the user does not control on the destination chain. The participation also stores the origin address, so claimRewards on the destination chain rejects the user whose msg.sender differs from participation.userAddress, leaving rewards locked.

## Broken invariants

- cross-chain destination assets must be sent to an address controlled by the user on the destination chain
- reward claimant identity must match the user account that can sign on the destination chain
- cross-chain campaign participation should not lock rewards because of chain-specific wallet address differences
