## Summary

Wise Lending 的 liquidation receive amount 计算在处理 uncollateralized/private collateral 时先消费了普通余额，过晚检查 uncollateralized 标志，使 liquidator 可以少付 shares 却拿走更多 collateral，增加坏账。

## Broken invariants

- liquidation payout must be bounded by the debt shares actually repaid
- private or uncollateralized balances must not be seized using pricing paths intended for normal collateral
