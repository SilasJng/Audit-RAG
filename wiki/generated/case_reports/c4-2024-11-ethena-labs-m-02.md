## Summary

Non-whitelisted users can redeem collateral tokens and burn their UStb even when whitelist mode has been enabled on UStb contract. This breaks the main invariant mentioned in the README.

## Broken invariants

- during WHITELIST_ENABLED state, collateral redemption that burns UStb must only consume tokens from whitelisted holders
- privileged redeemer execution must not bypass holder-level whitelist restrictions on the from address
- off-chain RFQ or redeemer mediation must not replace the on-chain invariant that non-whitelisted balances cannot be burned for redemption
