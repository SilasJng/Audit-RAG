## Summary

handleReallocation accepts a userAddress supplied through Li.Fi Executor calldata and does not verify that the transaction initiator is the actual campaign participant. An attacker can flash-borrow target tokens from a Uniswap V2 pool, route them through Executor into handleReallocation, and set userAddress to the pool address. NudgeCampaign sends the target tokens back to the pool in the same transaction, satisfying the flash swap, while registering a participation for the pool address. Because the pool continues to hold enough target tokens, off-chain balance monitoring may not invalidate it, allowing the attacker to consume or lock campaign reward allocation at only the flash-swap fee cost.

## Broken invariants

- campaign rewards should be allocated only to the real user who initiated and funded the reallocation
- a DEX pool receiving returned flash-liquidity should not count as a legitimate campaign participant
- off-chain balance monitoring should not be the only defense against arbitrary participant assignment
