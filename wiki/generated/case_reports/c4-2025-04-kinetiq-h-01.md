## Summary

When users stake into the Staking Manager and get their KHYPE tokens, after earning some rewards they might want to queue a withdrawal to get their HYPE tokens back. While the queued withdrawal delay is on, the user can decide to cancelWithdrawal and get their KHYPE tokens back. The way the buffer is handled in this flow leads to locking of HYPE in the staking manager contract.

## Broken invariants

- HYPE removed from hypeBuffer to satisfy a queued withdrawal must remain usable for withdrawal liquidity or be restored on cancellation
- cancelled withdrawals must not turn buffer-resident HYPE into untracked or incorrectly redelegated funds
- redelegating cancelled withdrawal amounts must use the correct L1/EVM movement path for where the HYPE actually resides
