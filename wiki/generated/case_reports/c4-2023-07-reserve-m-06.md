## Summary

The CurveStableMetapoolCollateral is intended for 2-fiattoken stable metapools. The metapoolToken coin0 is pairedToken and the coin1 is lpToken, e.g. 3CRV. And the config.chainlinkFeed should be set for paired token.

## Broken invariants

- collateral tryPrice must reject or bound composed prices when any paired token oracle is stale
- timeout handling in one leg of a metapool must not produce a huge still-valid price
- oracle freshness must propagate through composed Curve metapool pricing
