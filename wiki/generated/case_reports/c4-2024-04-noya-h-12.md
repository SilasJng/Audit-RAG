## Summary

When removing a holdingPosition that is not at the back of holdingPositions array, the holdingPositionId is incorrectly calculated. This will mess up the position index, and ultimately mess up TVL calculation.

## Broken invariants

- protocol accounting and authorization must remain consistent under the root cause: registry position removal uses calculatorConnector instead of ownerConnector, deriving the wrong holdingPositionId and corrupting position tracking
- oracle, TVL, and collateral valuation must use correctly scaled, fresh, and manipulation-resistant inputs
- privileged, owner, and license checks must be enforced on every value-moving path, not only on the nominal entry point
