## Summary

Monetrix credited Portfolio Margin supplied balances from the HyperCore 0x811 precompile as positive backing but did not subtract borrow liabilities from the same PM/Borrow-Lend state. The resulting totalBackingSigned(), surplus(), and distributableSurplus() could be overstated, allowing normal settlement to distribute phantom yield even when true PM net equity was negative.

## Broken invariants

- external strategy or PM account backing must be measured as net equity, not gross supplied assets only
- every liability field in an upstream account state that can reduce claimable backing must be subtracted before surplus gates authorize yield
- settlement gates must not distribute yield when a positive supplied balance is offset by larger borrow debt in the same external account
