## Summary

The Aave plugin is associated with an ever-increasing exchange rate. The earlier a user wraps the AToken, the more Static Atoken will be minted and understandably no slippage protection is needed.

## Broken invariants

- static aToken withdrawals must validate underlying redemption conditions before burning or transferring shares
- wrapper exits must not rely on assumptions about external Aave token behavior without protection
- withdrawal paths should preserve principal even when external yield-token state is abnormal
