## Summary

Prove that a vault/accountant/connector uses net external equity for backing, surplus, solvency, or yield gates rather than gross supplied/collateral balances.

## Assertions

- net-equity-aware backing is supplied_value - borrow_liability after matching units and prices
- surplus/distributable/solvency is non-positive when borrow liabilities exceed supplied assets
- the downstream value-moving gate reverts or refuses distribution under net-negative external state
- a vulnerable implementation reports positive backing or accepts settlement from the same state
